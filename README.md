# Receipt Shoebox Expense Capture Kit

**Stop retyping receipts every quarter.**

Log each receipt in ~2 minutes, keep the photo in one folder, and get your quarterly totals automatically. No cloud, no subscription, no uploads — everything stays on your device.

## The problem

Receipts pile up in a shoebox, a drawer, or a camera roll, and every quarter you sit down and retype them all from scratch. Slow, error-prone, and the reason expenses get reported late — or never.

## How it works

1. Log each receipt the day it arrives: date, vendor, category, amount, tax, payment method, and the photo's file name.
2. Keep the photo in one folder per year.
3. At quarter end, read the quarterly summary — totals are already computed.
